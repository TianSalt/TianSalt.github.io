# Yányǔwū Guide

## Style

Yányǔwū is written in [General American English](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/General_American_English) and follows [MLA style](https://style.mla.org/).

Curly quotation marks — `“”` and `‘’` — are used rather than [straight quotation marks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quotation_marks_in_English) (`"` and `'`).

## Romanization

Japanese terms on Yányǔwū are romanized according to [Wiktionary’s Japanese transliteration rules](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/Wiktionary:Japanese_transliteration), which are based on the [Hepburn system](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hepburn_romanization).

Where Wiktionary’s rules call for straight quotation marks, as in <i>ren'ai</i>, Yányǔwū uses curly ones instead: <i>ren’ai</i>.
